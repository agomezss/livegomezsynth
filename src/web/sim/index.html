Controls

  • K1-K4 patch parameters, K5-K7 navigation, K8 MASTER (drag a knob up/down or scroll on it).
  • KNOB BANK + FADER BANK together = the CHORD button below the buttons (a mouse has one pointer).
  • Pad LEDs, button LEDs and the screen are the firmware's.
