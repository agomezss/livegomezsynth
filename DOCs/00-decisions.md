| D-67 | **Note latency while the screen redraws.** Hardware report on 0.1.11 (PERFORM, 6 voices, render 2545 us avg / 4520 us peak of a 5805 us block, so not a render overrun): notes played with the right hand started to lag. | D-70 | **Drum pads play like the keys (0.1.14).** Hardware report: in PATCH with a drum kit the pads cut sounds and did not behave like the keys, which play the kit perfectly. Both go through `note_event_p` to the same engine, so the differences were the pad's own velocity (a soft touch gave a very quiet or silent hit, the kit's gain follows velocity squared) and a rebound (a second hit right after the release retriggers the lane and cuts its tail). A pad hit now has a fixed velocity (`DRUM_PAD_VEL` 100) and a hit within 40 ms of the same pad's release is ignored (`DRUM_PAD_GUARD_MS`). `tests/polyphony_test.mjs` checks both in the simulator; the rebound is a hypothesis from the code and the pad state machine, not seen on the device. |
